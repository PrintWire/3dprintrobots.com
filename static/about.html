About 3D Print Robots

Covering additive manufacturing for robotics additive manufacturing — from concept to production hardware.

OUR STORY

Robotics at the Frontier of Fabrication

3D Print Robots covers the full spectrum of additive manufacturing for robotics — from compliant grippers and lightweight arms to chassis, drone airframes, and the mechanisms that move them.

Our editorial team includes robotics engineers, mechanical designers, and materials scientists with real project experience across arms, mobile platforms, and end-of-arm tooling.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover robotics AM from the flexure gripper to the full chassis with equal depth — the mechanism, the structure, and the material behind them.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative, vendor-independent source for additive manufacturing in robotics — connecting builders to the suppliers reshaping how robots get made.

WHAT DRIVES US

What Sets Us Apart
